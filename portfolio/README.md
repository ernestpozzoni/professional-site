## Featured prototype case study

### Open Source, Open Standards and Digital Sovereignty Operating Model Proposal

**2024 · Client-context initiative developed during the Link Communications Systems engagement**

A strategic operating-model proposal for a resource-constrained satellite-connectivity SME, examining whether greater use of open-source software, open standards and UK- or EU-hosted services could reduce supplier dependency, strengthen data control and improve long-term operational ownership.

The work combined option assessment, software maturity evaluation, financial scenario modelling, governance analysis and regulatory/security considerations. It identified approximately **£5,000–£28,000 in potential three-year gross savings**, while explicitly separating those possible savings from implementation, migration, support, training and operational-management costs.
